## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **IRON NEST: Heavy Turret Simulator**. This trainer provides convenience options for the game's artillery-management loop, including ammunition control, faster mechanical operation, simplified firing preparation, and protection from operational setbacks. It is intended for the single-player campaign and related challenge content.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to explore the campaign, artillery systems, ammunition options, and tactical missions with reduced difficulty or greater freedom during offline single-player play.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

IRON NEST is experienced from inside the artillery fortress rather than through conventional battlefield traversal. Move between the operator areas and interact with the controls, map, teleprinters, gauges, loading systems, and mechanical stations required for each mission.

### Tools & Inventory

Ammunition is managed as part of the artillery workflow. Select an available shell type, load the gun, and monitor the remaining stock before committing to a firing sequence. Unlockable ammunition and abilities expand the options available during later operations.

### World & Environment

The campaign takes place in alternate-history Spain in the late 1920s, as the monarchy faces a spreading republican uprising. Orders arrive through teleprinters, frontline information must be interpreted, and targets are handled through an interactive tactical map across multiple operational regions.

### Combat & Weapons

Combat revolves around receiving coordinates, measuring positions on the tactical map, preparing a firing solution, operating the hydraulics, traversing the massive turret, loading the selected ammunition, and firing the guns. Accuracy and timing are essential because each shot consumes resources and battlefield events can create new operational pressure.
